How the Content-Based Filtering Works

01

Feature Engineering

Metadata from the real TMDB dataset (genres, plot overview, keywords, cast, and director) are concatenated into a consolidated descriptive document for each film.

02

TF-IDF Vectorization

Scikit-learn's TfidfVectorizer converts text descriptions into high-dimensional numerical vectors, penalizing ubiquitous stop words and weighting salient terminology.

03

Cosine Similarity

The cosine angle between the target film's vector and every other film vector is computed: $$\cos(\theta) = \frac{\mathbf{A} \cdot \mathbf{B}}{\|\mathbf{A}\| \|\mathbf{B}\|}$$ Films with the highest cosine scores are returned in descending order.
